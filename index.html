How it works

Your company, plugged into an AI team

No consultants, no months of setup. You describe how your business works — the AI team takes over the routine.

STEP 01

Register your company

Create a workspace, connect your channels — Telegram for daily work, web cabinet for control. Your data is isolated per company, by design.

STEP 02

Hire AI employees

Pick roles from the catalog: accountant assistant, document assistant, coordinator. Onboard them like real hires — share your templates, rules and contacts.

STEP 03

Approve, don't execute

AI employees prepare drafts, reconcile statements, draft contracts and daily summaries. Every action that matters waits for your explicit approval.

Why us

Born in a real back office — not in a pitch deck

AI Office wasn't designed as a demo. It grew out of the working back office of a live e-commerce transportation business, where AI employees have been reconciling bank statements and preparing documents every day since May 2026.

✓

Human-in-the-loop by design. AI prepares — a person approves. No autonomous payments, no silent actions.

Hard data isolation. Every company lives in its own isolated space, enforced at the database level.
